# Code of Conduct

CampusCore follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles: be respectful, inclusive, constructive, and focused on a harassment-free community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.
